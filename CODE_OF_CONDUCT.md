# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in NepaliCode respectful, constructive, inclusive, and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with empathy, assume good faith, provide actionable feedback, respect different viewpoints, and keep discussions focused on improving NepaliCode. Educational questions, beginner contributions, and thoughtful disagreement are welcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized language or attention, doxxing, trolling, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Public or private communication connected to the project is covered by this policy.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct. Reports should be made privately to the project maintainer through [@diwaskhatri](https://github.com/diwaskhatri) or the repository’s private communication channels.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not permitted.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed events, and other spaces where someone represents NepaliCode or NepaliSource.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community standard and tailored for the NepaliCode project.
